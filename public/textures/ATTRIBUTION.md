# Planet maps

Maps by **Solar System Scope / INOVE**, https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/.
Licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

Original 2K JPG maps: Sun, Mercury, Venus atmosphere, Earth day/night/clouds,
Moon, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, and stars + Milky Way.
Files are renamed for local use. Rendering adjusts brightness and color space;
the source images are otherwise unmodified. Saturn rings are generated geometry.
The maps use spacecraft imagery with artist-filled unmapped regions and enhanced colors.
